🧪 Vibe Projects_

Small, weird, hands-on ML experiments. Every demo below runs and trains entirely in your browser — no backend, no build step, and most of them with no libraries at all.

a side-quest log by Ashwani Bhat · source on GitHub

🔧D16

Harness Lab

The model is fixed — tune the harness around it. Step budgets, verifiers, observation shaping, typed errors: same model, 34% → 92% of tickets solved.

▶ play source ↗ agentic systems
⚖️D17

Judge & Jury

Build an LLM-as-a-Judge protocol and watch it crown the wrong model. Position, verbosity and self-preference bias — measured by counterfactual.

▶ play source ↗ eval design
🩹D2

Self-Healing Pixel Art

Draw a sprite. A tiny neural cellular automaton learns to grow it from a single pixel — then erase chunks of it and watch the wound close itself.

▶ play source ↗ trains in-browser
🎧D3

Listen to a Neural Net Learn

Training as a score: each layer is a drone voice detuned by its gradients. An out-of-tune orchestra that tunes itself as the loss falls. Headphones on.

▶ play source ↗ zero libraries
🤟D4

Webcam Sign-Language Recognizer

Teach your browser your signs: record a handful of samples, train in ~200ms, then fingerspell at the camera and watch it type. Nothing is uploaded.

▶ play source ↗ needs a webcam
🧠D6

A Photo in a 12KB Neural Net

Don't store pixels — store a function. A SIREN learns f(x,y) → color until it is your photo, then fights JPEG at the same byte budget.

▶ play source ↗ trains in-browser
💤D7

Pong Dreams

A world model watches Pong until it learns the physics — then the simulator switches off and you play inside the network's hallucination as it drifts.

▶ play source ↗ playable dream
🦠D11

Evolving Walkers

Blobs of springs and joints learn to walk with no brain and no backprop — just mutation and the rule that whoever crawls furthest gets to reproduce.

▶ play source ↗ pure evolution
