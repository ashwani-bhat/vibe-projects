Hold ● record while holding a sign steady in front of the camera — vary the angle and distance a little. 30–60 samples per sign works well. Retrain any time you add samples or signs. Holding a recognized sign steady for a second types it into the transcript.

How it works: MediaPipe Hands finds 21 hand landmarks in every webcam frame. Those get canonicalized — wrist to the origin, rotated so the wrist→knuckle bone points up, scaled to unit hand size, left hands mirrored — so the classifier sees the shape of your hand, not where it happens to be. A 63→32→K softmax network (hand-rolled backprop + Adam, no ML library) trains on your recorded samples in a few hundred milliseconds, with gaussian jitter standing in for webcam wobble. Predictions are smoothed over frames, and a sign held steadily above 85% confidence for ~1s gets typed. Your camera frames and samples never leave the page — there is no backend.
