The drone chord has one voice per layer, detuned by how hard that layer is being corrected. The melody plays one note per classified point — in-scale when the net is right, a sour semitone off when it's wrong, louder for bigger mistakes. When the chord settles pure and the melody goes quiet and consonant, it has learned.

What you're hearing: a 2-24-24-1 network (~700 parameters) trains live in your page, and every part of the sound is a real training signal. The drone is a just-intonation A-major chord — root plus one voice per weight layer, each detuned in proportion to that layer's gradient magnitude, so backprop literally plays the orchestra out of tune until learning stops. Timbre follows the loss: sawtooth rasp at coin-flip loss, warm triangle as it converges. The melody probes one training point per beat: hidden-layer activations pick the pitch on a pentatonic scale, wrong answers land a semitone off-scale, loudness is the size of the error, and tempo tracks the loss — frantic early, calm at the end. Chimes mark 75% / 90% / 97% accuracy. No samples, no libraries — pure Web Audio oscillators and a hand-rolled backprop in ~200 lines.
