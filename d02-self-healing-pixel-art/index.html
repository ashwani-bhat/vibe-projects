The preview shows the CA growing your sprite from one seed pixel. First it learns to grow (~300 steps), then samples get deliberately damaged so it learns to heal. ~1500–3000 steps gives solid regeneration. You can pause anytime.

How it works: every pixel is a little agent with 16 numbers of state. Each tick it peeks at its 3×3 neighborhood (Sobel filters), runs a 2-layer network, and nudges its own state. No pixel knows the whole picture — the image is a stable attractor of thousands of local conversations, which is why wounds close themselves. Based on Growing Neural Cellular Automata (Mordvintsev et al., Distill 2020). Everything runs and trains in your browser.
